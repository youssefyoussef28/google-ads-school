    Questions about the school

    Who writes the lessons?

    Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, researches and writes each one. Lessons link their sources, so check a change in your own account before acting on it.

    Does the school cost anything?

    No. Reading is free and there is no account to make.

    How is the school different from YouTube?

    Each lesson puts Google's claim, independent data and practitioner tests side by side, dated and sourced, and links the best videos on the topic.

    Which language are the lessons in?

    English only, for now.

    Can I argue with a lesson?

    Yes. Every lesson ends with an agree or disagree vote and a discussion, and a correction shows up in the changelog.

    The sources behind each lesson

    Google's own announcements and help pages come first, then the trade press, then practitioners who publish their tests. Forums only ever count as an early signal.
