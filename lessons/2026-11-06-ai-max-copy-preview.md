## In 30 seconds

- You can now **preview up to 10 sample AI-written headlines and descriptions** for a landing page **before** turning on AI Max text customization. Viewing them doesn't require opting in.
- The preview is a **sample**, not a promise: the live ads may use different text.
- Check each sample for **accuracy, brand voice and offer conditions** (like a "free delivery" claim that only applies over a minimum order). Use **text guidelines** to steer the writing.

## What changed

On **October 8, 2026**, Search Engine Land reported a feature called **text customization asset previews** in AI Max. Based on a summary by agency Delante, it works like this ([Delante](https://delante.co/google-ads-ai-max-copy-preview/)):

- After you enter a **final URL**, Google's AI reads the landing page and generates **up to 10** sample headlines and descriptions, typically in under 30 seconds.
- **You don't have to opt in** to view them.
- Previews respect your **text guidelines**, such as words and themes to avoid.
- It works in the languages text customization supports, and adult content is excluded.
- **Where:** in Search campaigns, under **Asset optimization** in the AI Max settings (also when editing an existing campaign). Enter the final URL, and if the field was empty, choose "Preview examples".
- After you turn text customization on, Google-generated copy is labeled **"Google AI"** in the "Added by" column, and the combination reporting shows which asset combinations served.

> I couldn't open Search Engine Land's original article, so the details above come from Delante's summary of it. Delante itself stresses the preview is only a sample. Check the screen in your own account.

## Why it matters

Until now, turning on AI-written text meant trusting Google blind, then reviewing the damage afterward. The preview moves the review **before** the decision. You can see what the AI makes of your landing page, and a bad sample is useful information too: it often means the *page* is unclear, not the AI.

## The lesson: the three parts of AI Max (and where text sits)

AI Max for Search bundles three separate features. Knowing which is which keeps you in control:

| Feature | What it does | Risk if unwatched |
|---|---|---|
| **Search term matching** | Matches your ads to searches beyond your keywords | Irrelevant searches (see [the search terms report lesson](lesson.html?l=2026-11-04-ai-max-search-terms-and-ad-combinations)) |
| **Text customization** | AI writes headlines and descriptions from your landing page | Wrong or exaggerated claims in your ads |
| **Final URL expansion** | Sends people to a different page on your site if it fits the search better | Landing on the wrong page, location or offer |

The preview only covers the **middle row**. It doesn't show you which searches you'd match or which pages people would land on. A good preview is not a green light for the whole package.

Also note a limit in Delante's summary: Google's documentation indicates pages with **limited content produce fewer assets**, and a longer page isn't automatically better. The AI writes from what the page says, so thin or vague pages give thin, vague copy.

## Worked example: an online mattress store

*Illustrative scenario, not a real account.*

**SleepWell** has a mattress category page. The owner opens the AI Max settings, enters the page URL and clicks **Preview examples**. Samples include:

| Sample headline or description | Check | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Free delivery on every mattress" | The store's free delivery only applies to orders over $500 | ❌ Misleading, fix with guidelines |
| "100-night sleep trial" | True, stated on the page | ✅ Keep |
| "Made-to-order in 2 days" | Only the premium range is made to order, and takes 10 days | ❌ Inaccurate |
| "Orthopedic mattresses for back pain" | The store doesn't make medical claims | ⚠️ Legal risk, exclude |
| "Compare 8 mattress types" | True, page has a comparison tool | ✅ Keep |

Result: **2 of 5 usable, 3 need action**. The owner has two choices:
1. **Don't enable text customization**, and keep writing ads by hand.
2. **Enable it with text guidelines** that exclude medical claims and require the delivery condition, then check the "Added by" column weekly.

Either choice is better than discovering "free delivery on every mattress" after a customer complains.

> [!TIP]
> Preview **more than one page**. Delante suggests comparing two pages with similar offers but different levels of detail. If the thin page gives poor samples, fixing the page may help every ad that points to it, even if you never enable AI-written text.

## What others are doing

- **Delante's review criteria** are a good starting list: does the copy accurately reflect the product, match your brand voice, and keep important offer conditions, such as minimum order values or the difference between in-stock and made-to-order items. They present these as review scenarios, not errors they observed ([Delante](https://delante.co/google-ads-ai-max-copy-preview/)).
- **Adalysis**, a Google Ads tool company, has published a video on how to write effective AI Max text guidelines, which is the control that shapes what the AI writes. It's below.
- **Location3's franchise test** ran AI Max inside experiments and turned off final URL expansion where wrong location pages were a risk ([Location3](https://location3.com/blog/ai-max-franchise-conversion-growth/)). That's the same kind of caution applied to a different AI Max feature.

## Where the experts disagree

**Google's view:** the preview adds transparency, and text customization saves time and finds messages advertisers might not write themselves.

**The skeptics' view:** a sample of 10 isn't the live output. Even Delante, which is positive about the feature, stresses that the preview doesn't guarantee those assets will appear. And while previews help you decide *whether* to start, you still need to monitor after launch.

**What nobody has shown yet:** whether AI-written text beats well-written human copy. The preview can't show which copy drives more sales, only what the AI would write.

**Our take:** Use the preview, because it costs nothing and no opt-in. If most samples are accurate on your best pages, enable text customization **in one campaign**, with text guidelines, and review the "Added by" column weekly. If the samples include claims you can't stand behind, stay manual, and fix the landing page.

## Do this today

1. Open a **Search campaign → Settings → AI Max → Asset optimization** and enter your best landing page URL.
2. Click **Preview examples** (no opt-in needed). Note how many of the samples you'd be happy to publish.
3. For each sample, check **accuracy, brand voice and offer conditions**.
4. If you'd enable text customization, write **text guidelines** first: words to avoid, claims to exclude, required conditions.
5. Preview a **second page** with a similar offer and compare the quality.
6. After enabling, check the **"Added by"** column weekly and remove or exclude any copy you don't want.
7. Decide on **final URL expansion** separately: the preview doesn't tell you where people will land.
